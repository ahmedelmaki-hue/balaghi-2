# Balaghi demo report photos

These are synthetic photos used only while the demo map is enabled. Live report previews use the report's `image_url` from the API. The three assets were generated with the built-in imagegen tool and resized to 512 × 512 JPEG for the app.

- `demo_pothole.jpg`: Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: compact demonstration thumbnail for a civic road-damage report inside the Balaghi mobile app. Primary request: a believable smartphone photo of one pothole in an asphalt road in Tripoli, Libya, near a curb. Composition: close-to-medium street-level view, pothole clearly visible near center and recognizable when cropped to a small square. Natural daylight, realistic road texture, documentary rather than staged. No people, no vehicles blocking the damage, no text, no logo, no watermark, no UI framing.
- `demo_water_leak.jpg`: Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: compact demonstration thumbnail for a civic water-leak report inside the Balaghi mobile app. Primary request: a believable smartphone photo of a small municipal water pipe leak at the edge of a paved street in Tripoli, Libya, with water visibly flowing across the pavement from the curb. Composition: close-to-medium street-level view, leak clearly visible near center and recognizable when cropped to a small square. Natural daylight, documentary rather than staged. No people, no text, no logo, no watermark, no UI framing.
- `demo_garbage.jpg`: Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: compact demonstration thumbnail for a civic garbage report inside the Balaghi mobile app. Primary request: a believable smartphone photo of a modest pile of household garbage bags and scattered litter near a sidewalk and curb in Tripoli, Libya. Composition: close-to-medium street-level view, garbage clearly visible near center and recognizable when cropped to a small square. Natural daylight, documentary rather than staged. No people, no text, no logo, no watermark, no UI framing.
